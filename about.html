Our story

Tradition on the table.
Heart in every detail.

Contour makes and distributes Mexican pastries inspired by the comfort of familiar recipes and the joy of sharing good food.

Who we are

Rooted in tradition.
Made for today.

We bring authentic Mexican pastry flavours to contemporary tables. Our pastries are prepared with attention to detail and are served in five-star restaurants across the nation.

We believe premium quality should still feel welcoming. Whether you discover Contour at a restaurant, share a box with friends, or order for a celebration, we want every experience to feel honest, dependable, and memorable.

Meet the team
