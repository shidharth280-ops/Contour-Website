The people behind Contour

Small team.
Big love for pastry.

Contour began with a simple idea: bring the warmth of Mexican pastry traditions to more tables without losing the care that makes them special. We’re a close-knit team of bakers, makers, and food lovers who believe good food brings people together.

Every batch is handled with attention to detail, respect for tradition, and a practical, down-to-earth approach to hospitality.
